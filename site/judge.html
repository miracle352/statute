Kane said passed. What did the page do?

A Kane CLI test tells you the steps ran. Its evidence pack also holds every request, cookie and console message the page made along the way. Write what the page must never do, and Statute checks the pack with plain code. Start with a recorded run, or drop in your own .evidence file. Packs are read in memory and never stored.

The same check on 13 big online pharmacies in 9 countries: on 3 of the 8 Kane could search, "pregnancy test" reached an advertising or marketing company, twice after refusing cookies. How it was run.

Expectations

One per line. Steps are the test's ## headings, in quotes.

Rules Statute knows
  • no request to a tracker after "Step"
  • no request to a third party
  • no visitor ID sent to a tracker
  • no tracker cookie before "Step"
  • what was typed in "Step" never leaves this site
  • text "jane@x.com" never leaves this site
  • value of $total never leaves this site
  • no server errors
  • no failed requests to this site
  • no console errors
  • value $total is at most $price plus $tax
  • clicks in "Cancel" at most clicks in "Subscribe"
